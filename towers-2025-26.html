How to read this

The playoff race, conference by conference. Each bar is a team's points — two for a win, one for an overtime or shootout loss (the paler end of the bar) — and the faint stripes run on to the most it can still reach.

  • The dashed line is the playoff cut if the season ended now: the top three of each division (A1, M2, C3…) and two wild cards (WC1, WC2) per conference. Spots and the line appear once every team has played.
  • A team fades once eight teams already have more points than it can still reach — it is certainly out. On the latest night, a filled tag means the league has confirmed a clinched spot.
  • The slider replays the race game by game; hover a team for its record, games left and its distance from the line.
  • Bracket shows the playoffs, round by round, to the Stanley Cup.
