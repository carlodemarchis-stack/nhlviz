How to read this

Every team's season as a shape. All 32 share one baseline: wins build upward, losses hang downward, and games still to play hang from the ceiling.

  • An overtime or shootout loss is a half-height, faded box above the line: it earns one point where a win earns two. So the height of everything above the line is exactly the team's points — the standings, drawn.
  • Every box is to scale across the page, and the slider replays the season game by game while the teams re-sort live by points (then regulation wins).
  • A team's colour is its own, except where the primary is near-black and the secondary reads better.
  • Bracket shows the playoffs, round by round, to the Stanley Cup.
