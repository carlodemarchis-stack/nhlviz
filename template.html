How to read this

The __SEASON__ NHL season as a film: the leading scorers, the goalies with the most wins, the charts that rank them, then every team in order of points. Every card deep-links: copy the address bar.

  • Team cards — the curve is the season arc: points above a .500 pace after every game. A win lifts it, a regulation loss drops it, and an overtime or shootout loss — which still earns a point — leaves it level.
  • Skater cards — the bars are points in each game, split into goals, primary and secondary assists. Switch to Avg for at or above / below his own average, with his best night in gold. Skaters are ordered by points, goals breaking ties.
  • The rink is every located shot he took, turned so they all fly at the net on the right: goals filled, saved shots as rings, misses as faint dots. On a goalie's card it is every shot he faced, with the goals that beat him in red.
  • Goalie cards — the bars are shots faced, split into saves and goals against, and the result strip is his own decision, not the team's.
  • Unlisted cards — everyone else who played has a card too, reached from a roster tile, a name anywhere in the film, the search, or its number in the address bar. The arrows never wander into them.
